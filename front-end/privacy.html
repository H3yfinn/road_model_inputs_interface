Privacy notice — APERC Road Model Archive

Last updated: 27 August 2026

What this service does

The Road Model lets researchers prepare road-transport model inputs. When a researcher changes inputs and runs the model, the service may save a complete submission CSV and small metadata record to the designated Google Drive archive.

Google Drive access

The archive owner connects Google Drive once using OAuth. The service requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which permits it to create and manage the archive folder and files it uses. It does not request broad access to browse or manage all files in the archive owner's Drive.

The OAuth refresh token and client secret are stored as private deployment secrets. They are not sent to researchers using the model.

Information stored

An archived submission can include the submitted model values, comments, source/provenance labels, selected economy, model-input version, run identifier, timestamp, submission filename/identifier, and checksum. It is retained in the Google Drive archive until the archive administrator deletes it.

Sharing and access

The archive folder may be shared as Anyone with the link — Viewer so former colleagues can download submissions. Anyone who obtains that link can access the archive files. Researchers should therefore not include personal, confidential, or sensitive information in model comments or submissions.

Contact and choices

For archive questions or deletion requests, contact finn.maunsell@gmail.com. The archive owner can revoke the service's Google Drive access through their Google Account at any time.
